## What it is

A `preBuilt` campaign level flagged `sandbox: true` + `alwaysUnlocked: true`,
with a live knob panel. No objectives, no win, no loss, no clock. Every
crisis in the rest of the game is on a random or scripted schedule; here they
are on buttons.

## Two structural facts, built against

1. **Zero objectives = instant WIN.** `tickCampaign`'s sweep starts
   `allDone = true`, so an empty list is vacuously complete. The Lab
   therefore declares `objectives: []` *and* `sandbox: true`, and the flag is
   what stops it — proven by running 360 s past a finite 120 s `endsAt` and
   by mutation M1.
2. **`isLevelUnlocked` only frees index 0.** `alwaysUnlocked: true` opts the
   Lab out. It is placed last in `levelOrder()` so nothing is downstream of
   it, and a structural test asserts no `alwaysUnlocked` level is ever
   another level's predecessor — move it and the suite goes red (M15).

A third fact surfaced during design and is handled: with resolution skipped,
a `gameOver` (reputation reaches the floor after ~70 s of a dark room) would
have frozen `timeScale` behind *no modal at all* — a soft-lock. `tickDemand`
declines to set `gameOver` while `STATE.lab.on`. The floors are scoped, not
deleted: the same 100 s ends survival and ends an ordinary level, and both
controls are in the suite.

## Concerns / notes for review

1. **The brownout button does not visibly bite in the handed-over room.** The
   room's single 16 kW PDU caps the feed's load below the 20 kW a 50 % sag
   leaves, so the sag changes nothing until the player adds a second bus. That
   is the brownout's own documented lesson ("a facility drawing under half its
   feed capacity does not even notice"), and the banner and pill still fire —
   but it is the one button whose effect needs a build to see. The test builds
   that second bus explicitly and proves the clip is real.
2. **`timeLimitSec: 120` on a level with no clock** is deliberate and
   commented, but it is a value that means nothing at runtime. It exists so
   the machine test can prove the flag, not the number, is what stops
   resolution.
3. **Duration midpoints.** `brownout`, `outage` and `tariff` have ranged
   durations; sim modules may not roll dice, so the Lab fires the midpoint of
   the CONFIG range. Documented in `lab.js`.
4. **Pre-existing debug logging** in `src/i18n.js`'s `setLocale` prints the
   locale and the entire translation table to the console on every language
   switch. Not mine, not touched — flagged as a separate one-line PR.
5. No new tick function was added, so there is no new `dt <= 0` / NaN /
   Infinity surface; the existing guards in `tickCampaign`, `tickDemand` and
   `tickHeat` are unchanged.
